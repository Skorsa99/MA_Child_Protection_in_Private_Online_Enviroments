Child Protection in Private Online Eviroments

This page is part of the Master’s Thesis by Samuel Skorsetz, which aims to improve child protection in private online environments by developing a nudity-detection AI model that runs fully in the user’s browser.

The goal of the project is to enable real-time detection of unsafe visual content during video calls without sending any images or videos to external servers. This ensures strong privacy, especially in scenarios involving children.

  • The model runs entirely on the child’s device (no data leaves the browser).
  • It classifies images into safe, unsafe, and empty.
  • Optimized for real-time use, even on low- to mid-range hardware.
  • Designed for video-call platforms, online tutoring, and similar use cases.
  • Built on a large, partially manually verified dataset to ensure reliable accuracy.

By clicking the “Test the Model” button below, you will be taken to an interactive demo where you can activate your camera or upload images and see how the model classifies them—including a preview of how the model interprets your input.
